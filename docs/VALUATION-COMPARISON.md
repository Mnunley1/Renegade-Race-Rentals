## 5. Implications for deal math

If both sides close at base-case valuations:

| | GridSeat | Renegade equity | Combined |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-deal value | $2.25M | $2.75M | **$5.0M** |
| Share of combined | ~45% | ~55% | 100% |

If GridSeat acquires Renegade but valuations are comparable:

- Renegade shareholders should receive **~50–55%** of post-close GridSeat — not a small rollover stake.
- The **$75k–$150k deferred cash** remains a separate liquidity leg; it does not replace equity at this relative-value range.
- GridSeat's **existing investors/advisors** (NDA Exhibit A) will dilute everyone — cap table diligence is required before locking percentages.

**Illustrative post-close cap table (before deferred cash payout / new raise):**

| Holder | Approx. % |
|---|---|
| Renegade founders + angel (rolled in) | ~52–55% |
| Mario / GridSeat existing | ~35–40% |
| GridSeat existing investors | ~5–10% (unknown until diligence) |

This supports a **board seat and meaningful operating role** for Renegade's founder — roughly half the combined value is being contributed by Renegade, not acquired as a bolt-on.

## 7. Diligence items that move GridSeat's number ±$500k+

4. **Growth rate** — if GMV doubled in 6 months, base case moves toward **$2.5M–$3.0M**
5. **ChampCar naming rights** — exclusive? Revenue share? Duration?
