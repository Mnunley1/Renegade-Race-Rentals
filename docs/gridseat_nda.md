**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date last signed below ("Effective Date") by and between:

**Party A:** Orion Speed LLC, a Florida limited liability company doing business as "GridSeat," with its principal place of business in Winter Springs, Florida ("GridSeat").

**Party B:** RaceRentals, LLC, represented by Levi Goffeney ("Renegade Race Rentals").

Each referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

**Recitals**

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship, which may include but is not limited to acquisition, merger, partnership, technology integration, or other commercial arrangements (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information. The Parties wish to protect such information as set forth in this Agreement.

**1\. Definition of Confidential Information**

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

**a.** Business plans, strategies, financial information, revenue data, pricing models, and projections.

**b.** User data, customer lists, driver databases, team owner information, and usage metrics.

**c.** Technology, source code, algorithms, system architecture, trade secrets, and intellectual property.

**d.** Contracts, partnerships, vendor agreements, and business relationships.

**e.** Product roadmaps, feature plans, and unreleased product information.

**f.** The existence and terms of this Agreement and the discussions between the Parties regarding the Purpose.

**2\. Exclusions**

Confidential Information does not include information that:

**a.** Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party.

**b.** Was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure, as demonstrated by written records.

**c.** Is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of or reference to the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**d.** Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure.

**e.** Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided the receiving Party gives the disclosing Party prompt written notice and cooperates in seeking a protective order.

**3\. Obligations**

Each Party agrees to:

**a.** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

**b.** Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose described in this Agreement and for no other purpose.

**c.** Limit access to Confidential Information to those employees, advisors, and representatives who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

**d.** Protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

**e.** Promptly notify the disclosing Party of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

**4\. Term**

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and shall remain in force for a period of six (6) months (the "Term"). The confidentiality obligations set forth in Section 3 shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement for an additional period of twelve (12) months following the end of the Term.

**5\. Non-Circumvention**

For the Term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following its expiration or termination, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, contact, deal with, or otherwise become involved with any person, entity, or business relationship (including but not limited to racing series, track owners, sponsors, or vendors) introduced or disclosed by the other Party, for the purpose of avoiding any obligation to the other Party or for any commercial benefit that bypasses the other Party, without the express prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

In the event that a Party can demonstrate that a pre-existing business relationship existed with such third party prior to the Effective Date, the restrictions in this Section shall not apply to the specific scope of that pre-existing relationship. To facilitate clarity, each Party shall provide the other with a list of its existing key business relationships as of the Effective Date, to be attached as Exhibit A (GridSeat) and Exhibit B (Renegade Rentals) to this Agreement. Omission from such lists shall not waive the pre-existing relationship defense, provided the Party can otherwise demonstrate the relationship through written records.

**6\. Return or Destruction of Information**

Upon the expiration or termination of this Agreement, or upon written request by the disclosing Party, the receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information received from the disclosing Party, including all copies, summaries, and derivative works. The receiving Party shall confirm destruction in writing if requested. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the receiving Party may retain copies as required by law or for internal compliance purposes, subject to the ongoing confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

**7\. No Obligation**

This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement, transaction, or business relationship. Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any decision not to proceed with the Purpose. Each Party reserves the right to terminate discussions at any time for any reason.

**8\. No License**

Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license, right, or interest in the other Party's intellectual property, trademarks, patents, copyrights, or trade secrets, except the limited right to review Confidential Information for the Purpose.

**9\. Non-Solicitation**

During the Term and for six (6) months following expiration or termination of this Agreement, neither Party shall directly solicit or attempt to hire any employee, contractor, or key business relationship of the other Party that was identified or introduced through the Confidential Information or discussions under this Agreement.

**10\. Remedies**

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, either Party may seek injunctive or equitable relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the need to post a bond.

**11\. Governing Law**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved in the courts of Seminole County, Florida.

**12\. Entire Agreement**

This Agreement, including the attached Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

**13\. Counterparts**

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Electronic or digital signatures shall be deemed valid and binding.
