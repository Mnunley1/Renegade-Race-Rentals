# Renegade Race Rentals — Business Valuation & Investment Summary

_Prepared for prospective acquirers and strategic investors · Confidential_

---

## 1. Executive Investment Thesis

**Renegade Race Rentals** is a specialized motorsport marketplace and digital platform focused on high-value track-day rentals, coaching, driver services, and performance automotive commerce. The Company is positioned at the intersection of peer-to-peer asset rentals, premium experiential motorsport, and specialized enthusiast e-commerce.

The acquisition opportunity is not a generic early-stage marketplace. Renegade provides an operating foundation in a niche where trust, access, safety, supply quality, and relationship credibility are significant barriers to entry. Motorsport participation is highly relationship-driven, operationally complex, and materially underserved by broad consumer rental platforms. Renegade is purpose-built for this environment.

The Company's platform and commercial position are supported by several core assets:

- **250 registered users** in a targeted motorsport and performance automotive audience.
- **10 track-day rental cars** available through the platform, with daily rental pricing ranging from **$900 to $9,500 per day**.
- **Exclusive rental rights** for all rentals at Private Driving Events, representing **10 private track events per year** across premier venues including **Watkins Glen, VIR, Monticello Motor Club, and Road Atlanta**.
- Approximately **$30,000 of projected GMV over the next two months**, demonstrating near-term transaction visibility.
- **5 professional driving coaches** ready to onboard into the platform.
- A defined product roadmap for **The Paddock**, a motorsport social feed, and **The Grid**, a race team page where teams can list openings for drivers, coaches, photographers, and other service providers.
- Planned platform expansion into event support, custom invoicing, customizable e-signable waivers, and in-app insurance purchasing.
- An anticipated full platform release this fall, supported by a prepared social media campaign designed by a social media expert.
- A business model designed to expand beyond rentals into coaching, event services, memberships, insurance/referral revenue, performance commerce, and social/networking features for the motorsport community.

The strategic value of Renegade is driven by its ability to aggregate fragmented motorsport demand into a dedicated marketplace with controlled supply, trusted operators, and exclusive event-level access. A competitor would need to replicate the platform, source high-value rental inventory, establish trust with vehicle owners and drivers, secure event relationships, onboard qualified coaches, and build demand in a market where credibility is earned slowly.

Renegade shortens that timeline. For a strategic acquirer, the Company offers immediate market entry, active supply, early customer data, premium event channels, and a scalable foundation for a larger motorsport services platform.

## 2. Valuation Methodology & Justification

### Proposed Valuation

**Indicative enterprise value: $750,000**

Renegade is currently best valued using an **Asset + Strategic Platform Premium** methodology. Because the Company is pre-revenue, a conventional EBITDA multiple is not the appropriate primary framework. The proposed valuation reflects the cost, time, execution risk, and commercial scarcity associated with assembling the Company's platform, supply base, user network, event access, and motorsport-specific operating relationships.

The valuation is supported by four principal value categories:

1. **Marketplace infrastructure** — a purpose-built platform for high-value motorsport rentals, coaching, and related services.
2. **Controlled rental supply** — 10 available track-day rental cars priced from $900 to $9,500 per day.
3. **Exclusive event access** — rental rights across 10 annual Private Driving Events at nationally recognized road courses.
4. **Near-term transaction visibility** — approximately $30,000 of projected GMV within the next two months.
5. **Defined fall release roadmap** — planned launch of The Paddock social feed, The Grid race team page, event support, custom invoices, e-signable waivers, and in-app insurance purchasing.

The daily pricing profile of the 10-car rental supply is a meaningful valuation driver. At full single-day deployment, the platform's current rental supply represents a theoretical gross booking capacity of approximately **$9,000 to $95,000 per event day**, depending on vehicle mix, utilization, and pricing. Across the 10 exclusive annual private events, this creates a clear pathway for recurring event-driven GMV before adding coaching, memberships, service fees, insurance/referral economics, or performance commerce.

The proposed $750,000 valuation should therefore be understood as a platform-entry valuation rather than a historical earnings valuation. It represents the value of acquiring an operational foundation in a specialized, high-spend market before transaction volume is fully mature.

### Why The Valuation Is Defensible

Renegade's value is not derived from current earnings. It is derived from a combination of strategic control points that are difficult to reproduce:

- Access to premium rental inventory in a high-value category.
- Event-level rental rights that concentrate qualified demand.
- A curated user base in a narrow enthusiast market.
- Coach supply ready for monetization.
- A platform model with multiple revenue layers beyond the initial rental transaction.
- A defined product roadmap that extends the platform from rental marketplace into social, team staffing, event operations, insurance, and B2B workflow infrastructure.
- A brand and digital presence that can serve as infrastructure for a broader motorsport marketplace.

For an acquirer already operating in motorsport, automotive services, track events, performance retail, specialty insurance, or enthusiast media, Renegade can accelerate entry into an adjacent revenue channel without starting from zero.

## 4. Risk Mitigation & Operational Transition

Renegade's operating profile is designed to mitigate several common risks associated with motorsport and specialty automotive businesses.

### Marketplace Model Reduces Inventory Burden

The business is structured around marketplace access and controlled supply rather than a capital-intensive inventory model. This reduces the need to carry large volumes of specialized parts, fuel, or depreciating physical stock. The platform can scale through vehicle owners, coaches, event organizers, and service providers while maintaining a comparatively asset-light operating structure.

### Event Rights Concentrate Qualified Demand

The exclusive rental rights at 10 annual Private Driving Events provide structured demand access. Rather than relying solely on broad digital advertising, Renegade is connected to environments where participants already have intent, budget, and a need for high-quality rental and coaching services.

### Premium Pricing Supports Margin Potential

Daily rental prices ranging from $900 to $9,500 create a high-value transaction base. Even modest take rates or service fees can produce meaningful revenue at scale. Additional services such as coaching, concierge support, memberships, insurance referrals, featured listings, and commerce can expand gross margin beyond the base rental transaction.

### Technology And Platform Updates

The platform model allows the acquirer to enhance functionality over time without changing the core market position. Product improvements may include enhanced listing workflows, digital waivers, payments, insurance verification, calendar management, coach booking, user profiles, social features, and event-specific storefronts.

### Founder Transition And Ongoing Role

Founder involvement is a negotiable transaction item and can be structured to match the acquirer's preferred operating model. Renegade can be transferred as a clean asset acquisition with a defined handoff period, or the founder can remain involved as a long-term partner, operator, advisor, or commercial lead.

This flexibility is a benefit to the acquirer. A buyer seeking immediate control can receive the platform, relationships, documentation, and operating assets without requiring indefinite founder dependency. A buyer seeking continuity, market knowledge, and relationship leverage can retain the founder in an ongoing role to accelerate post-closing growth.

Potential transition structures include:

- **Clean handoff:** Full transfer of assets, platform access, records, relationships, and operating materials at closing, with limited post-closing support as agreed.
- **Defined transition period:** 60- to 90-day founder support period to assist with relationship transition, user communication, supplier onboarding, and platform continuity.
- **Extended advisory role:** Founder remains available for strategic support, introductions, market guidance, and event/channel development.
- **Operating partner role:** Founder remains actively involved in growth, partnerships, event relationships, supply development, and marketplace execution.
- **Long-term strategic partnership:** Founder continues indefinitely as a partner to support expansion, commercial execution, and brand development, subject to mutually agreed economics and governance.

Core transition components include:

- Assignment and documentation of all platform assets, domain assets, user data, brand materials, and operating records.
- Formal transfer or re-documentation of exclusive rental rights with Private Driving Events.
- Direct introductions to event contacts, vehicle suppliers, and professional coaches.
- Migration of platform credentials, analytics, administrative access, payment infrastructure, and customer records.
- Buyer-approved communication plan to users, partners, and suppliers.
- Customary non-solicitation, confidentiality, and non-compete protections, subject to final transaction structure.
- Optional earnout, retained equity, consulting agreement, employment agreement, or partnership structure tied to GMV, revenue, coach activation, event utilization, or expansion milestones.

The purpose of this framework is to preserve maximum transaction flexibility. Renegade can be delivered as a turn-key platform with a clean transfer of control, or it can be paired with continued founder participation where the acquirer determines that long-term involvement would create additional strategic value.

## 5. Growth Catalysts

### 1. Fall Platform Release And Launch Campaign

Renegade has a defined roadmap toward a full platform release this fall. The release plan expands the Company beyond a rental marketplace into a broader motorsport operating platform with social, team, event, insurance, and service-provider functionality.

Key roadmap components include:

- **The Paddock:** a motorsport social feed designed to increase user engagement, content creation, community discovery, and repeat platform usage.
- **The Grid:** a race team page where teams can list available openings for drivers, coaches, photographers, and other service providers.
- **Event support:** tools to support event organization, participant coordination, and track-day service workflows.
- **Custom invoices:** configurable invoicing for rentals, coaching, team services, event packages, and related transactions.
- **Customizable e-signable waivers:** digital waiver workflows intended to reduce administrative friction and support event/rental compliance.
- **In-app insurance purchasing:** integrated insurance purchasing capability designed to simplify risk management for operators and participants.

Upon full release, Renegade has a social media campaign ready to deploy that was designed by a social media expert. If the Company is not acquired prior to full launch, Renegade intends to begin a heavy marketing push to accelerate user acquisition, supply onboarding, and event-driven transaction volume.

For an acquirer, this creates a clear timing advantage: a buyer can acquire the platform before full public release and control the launch strategy, or acquire into a business that is prepared to begin aggressive market activation.

### 2. Expansion Across Premium Track Markets

The current event footprint provides a replicable model for expansion into additional track-heavy regions. The acquirer can extend the marketplace into other premium road course markets, private clubs, manufacturer driving programs, track-day organizations, and performance driving schools.

Priority expansion markets may include the Northeast, Southeast, Texas, California, Midwest, and other regions with dense enthusiast and club-racing communities.

### 3. B2B Partnerships With Event Organizers, Clubs, Tracks, And OEM Programs

Renegade can serve as a white-label or preferred rental and services layer for event organizers that do not want to build their own technology or manage rental supply directly. Potential partners include:

- Private driving event organizers.
- Track-day operators.
- Motorsports clubs.
- Driving schools.
- Race teams.
- Exotic rental operators.
- OEM and dealer performance programs.
- Corporate hospitality and client entertainment programs.

These partnerships can create recurring demand channels while increasing platform credibility and utilization.

### 4. Coaching, Concierge, And High-Margin Services

The onboarding pipeline of 5 professional driving coaches creates an immediate path to service revenue. Coaching can be bundled with rentals, sold as a standalone product, or packaged into premium event experiences.

Additional high-margin service layers may include:

- Coach booking commissions.
- Premium driver memberships.
- Featured vehicle listings.
- Event concierge services.
- Digital waivers and compliance workflows.
- Insurance and referral fees.
- Data profiles for drivers, vehicles, and event participation.

These service layers improve monetization without requiring proportional increases in physical inventory.

### 5. Performance Commerce And Community Expansion

Once rental and coaching demand is active, Renegade can expand into specialized e-commerce and social features for the motorsport community. The platform can support commerce around safety gear, tires, fluids, performance parts, data systems, apparel, event preparation packages, and partner vendor offerings.

The social and community layer can further strengthen retention by giving drivers, coaches, teams, and vehicle owners a place to maintain profiles, build credibility, promote availability, and transact around future events.

## Conclusion

Renegade Race Rentals offers a strategic acquirer or investor an early position in a specialized, affluent, and fragmented motorsport market. The proposed **$750,000 valuation** is supported by controlled access to 10 premium track-day rental cars, exclusive event-level rental rights, 250 registered users, near-term GMV visibility, coach supply, and a purpose-built marketplace model.

The Company is pre-revenue, but it is not pre-infrastructure. Renegade provides the acquirer with a foundation that would require meaningful time, relationship capital, technical development, and market credibility to reproduce independently.

For the right acquirer, Renegade can become a scalable platform for motorsport rentals, coaching, event services, performance commerce, and enthusiast community infrastructure.
