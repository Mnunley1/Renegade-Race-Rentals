How to use this in the negotiation

Talking points by Gridseat objection
"We can't commit to that principal — it's too much."
Drop principal in $25k increments. The framework still works at $50k; it's the protection structure that matters more than the dollar amount.
"We don't want to commit a percentage of our next raise."
Drop the percentage formula entirely — make the payment unconditional on qualifying-round close. Keeps the deferral but removes the formula. Use this concession to hold the line on the ratchet.
Step down to 1.5× before dropping the ratchet entirely. The ratchet is what makes future Gridseat investors care about this clause in their own diligence. Don't give it up for free.
"We can't accept default interest stepping to 18%."
Standard commercial default rate is 12–18%. Drop to 12% but keep the acceleration triggers. The step-up exists to discourage default, not punish a slow payment.
"What if we never raise? We can't have this hanging forever."
