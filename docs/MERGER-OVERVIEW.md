# Renegade Race Rentals — Proposed Combination with Gridseat

_Prepared May 23, 2026 — confidential, under our existing mutual NDA._

---

## 1. Executive summary

We'd like to propose Gridseat acquire Renegade Race Rentals and fold the team, platform, and partnerships into Gridseat. We're proposing this structure (vs. a merger-of-equals or Renegade-as-acquirer) because Gridseat already has the operating brand and customer base we'd otherwise spend the next year building, and our assets — the platform, exclusivities, venue partnership, and insurance carrier deal — plug in cleanly to accelerate what you're already doing.

Three terms we feel strongly about and need to surface up front:

1. **Board seat with voting rights** for me at Gridseat — not observer-only. This is a requirement, not a negotiating ask.
2. **A defined ongoing role for me at Gridseat** — flexible on advisory vs. full-time operating, depending on what Gridseat needs and what makes sense for the combined org. Title, scope, and time commitment to be worked out together.
3. **Deferred-cash component with enforceable payment terms.** We understand Gridseat doesn't have cash available today. Total consideration is **equity ($2.5M–$3.0M for Renegade's assets) plus deferred cash ($75k–$150k on top for founder liquidity and debt payoff)** — two separate buckets, not equity OR cash for the same dollars. The cash portion pays out when Gridseat raises seed funding, documented as a binding obligation with real consequences for non-payment (interest accrual + equity ratchet + acceleration on default). The mechanics are in Section 7.

Everything else is open for discussion. The rest of this document covers what Renegade brings, what we believe it's worth, the small known liabilities to clear at close, and a proposed framework for the deal so we can move directly to LOI.

## 6. Why combining the two companies makes sense

Most motorsport-services startups fail for one of three reasons: (1) supply concentration, (2) insurance, (3) running out of operational depth before brand reaches escape velocity. Combining Gridseat and Renegade is materially stronger on all three than either of us independently — and Renegade has already solved (2).

**What Renegade brings into Gridseat on day one.**

- Built, production marketplace platform — saves 12–18 months of engineering at a typical seed-stage team's burn rate
- Insurance carrier partnership with annual-policy product for arrive-and-drive operators — recurring, high-margin commission revenue plus the moat that has historically killed peer competitors. **Your existing operator base could be offered this product immediately.**
- Venue partnership via Uwharrie Motorsports Park co-founder
- Exclusive deal flow from a race team and a private driving organizer
- 3+1 GT4 supply
- Roadmap and execution capacity for race-data integrations (race-monitor, iRacing) and the Paddock social/networking layer — both unlock data and content advantages Gridseat doesn't have today
- 280-driver pre-launch demand list

**What Gridseat brings that we don't have.**

- Operating brand and customer recognition we'd otherwise spend a year building
- Existing customer base and operator relationships at scale beyond our exclusivities
- Operational depth on customer service, event ops, and the broader services layer
- Capital position and runway

The thesis is simple: a single combined company with shared supply, shared demand, a single platform, embedded insurance, and Gridseat's brand is substantially more valuable than the sum of the two independent businesses. Synergies are front-loaded — supply consolidation is immediate, the insurance product can be offered to Gridseat's operator base on day one, and a single engineering roadmap means no parallel work.

## 7. Proposed deal framework

We're proposing **Gridseat acquires 100% of Renegade Race Rentals** at close. Total consideration is structured in two parts:

| Bucket | Amount (illustrative) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Gridseat equity** | $2.5M–$3.0M of Renegade value | Purchase price for Renegade's assets — platform, IP, contracts, relationships, demand |
| **Deferred cash** | $75k–$150k | Founder liquidity, ~$10k debt payoff, minimum cash basis — **on top of** the equity valuation |
| **Total** | ~$2.6M–$3.15M | Equity + cash; not "equity OR cash" for the same dollars |

Gridseat gets **everything at close** for $0 cash. The deferred cash is paid later when Gridseat raises — it settles an obligation from the acquisition, it does not buy Renegade a second time.

### 7a. What Gridseat gets at close (before any deferred cash is paid)

When the merger closes, Gridseat owns 100% of Renegade. Renegade ceases to exist as a separate entity. Gridseat receives:

| Asset | Detail |
|---|---|
| Platform | Production marketplace — listings, booking, Stripe Connect, messaging, profiles, coach product, admin |
| IP | All software, branding, domains, product roadmap |
| Contracts | Race team exclusivity, private driving organizer exclusivity, GT4 supply relationships |
| Insurance partnership | Carrier deal for annual arrive-and-drive policies |
| Venue relationship | Uwharrie Motorsports Park co-founder relationship |
| Demand | 280-driver pre-launch waitlist |
| Team | Retention of founder, Malcom Nunley (CTO), and Uwharrie co-founder per terms below |

**In exchange at close:** Renegade shareholders receive Gridseat equity sized to $2.5M–$3.0M of Renegade value (expressed as a percentage of Gridseat post-acquisition fully-diluted). **$0 cash changes hands at close.**

### 7b. What the deferred cash pays for (when Gridseat raises)

The deferred-cash obligation is **not** Gridseat buying Renegade again. Renegade is already Gridseat's. The deferred cash is the **cash leg of total consideration** — deferred because Gridseat has no cash today.

| What the cash covers | Why it's separate from equity |
|---|---|
| ~$10k credit-card debt | Renegade liability assumed or paid off at raise |
| Founder liquidity | Modest cash for founders who've been building unpaid |
| Minimum cash basis | Cash component of a fair total deal; equity alone doesn't cover living expenses |

When Gridseat's seed investors ask *"What is this $100k obligation?"* the answer is: **deferred seller consideration from the Renegade acquisition.** Paying it now clears the obligation and avoids a 2× equity ratchet that would dilute those investors.

### 7c. Consideration summary

| Component | When | Amount | What it represents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gridseat equity | At close | Sized to $2.5M–$3.0M Renegade value | Asset purchase price — platform, contracts, IP, relationships |
| Cash at close | At close | $0 | Accommodating Gridseat's current cash position |
| Deferred cash | Within 30 days of $1M+ raise | $75k–$150k (to be agreed) | Separate cash leg — liquidity, debt, minimum cash basis |
| Earn-out (optional) | Milestone-based | TBD | Bridges any remaining valuation gap if needed |

**Example at midpoint:** $2.75M equity + $100k deferred cash = **$2.85M total consideration** to Renegade shareholders.

### 7d. Deferred-cash obligation — terms and protections

This is the section we feel strongly about. We're willing to defer cash, but the deferral must be a real, binding obligation with consequences for non-payment. Suggested terms (open to negotiation on amount and triggers, less open on the protection structure):

- **Principal amount.** To be agreed in the LOI — order of magnitude $75k–$150k total. This covers (i) the ~$10k credit-card debt assumed/rolled in (Section 4), (ii) modest founder cash for the founders who have been operating Renegade unpaid, and (iii) Renegade founders' minimum cash basis in the transaction.
- **Payment trigger.** Within **30 days** of Gridseat closing its next qualified equity financing, defined as a priced round of **$1.0M or more** in primary capital from institutional or accredited investors.
- **Payment formula.** Lesser of (a) the full deferred principal, or (b) **15% of net round proceeds** — with a floor: if the round closes above $2.0M, the full deferred amount is paid in cash regardless of percentage.
- **Interest.** Accrues at **8% per annum** simple, starting 12 months after closing of the merger. Standard market protection against indefinite deferral.
- **Default penalties.** If the deferred amount is not paid within **24 months** of merger close, or within **90 days** of a qualifying round closing (whichever is earlier):
  - Unpaid balance converts to additional Gridseat preferred equity at **50% of the price per share** of the most recent priced round (a 2× equity ratchet — strong incentive to pay rather than convert).
  - Default interest rate steps up to **18%** until the obligation is satisfied.
  - Renegade founders gain a **board observer seat** in addition to my voting seat until paid.
- **Acceleration triggers.** Entire balance becomes immediately due and payable (with default interest) on:
  - Change of control of Gridseat (sale, merger, recapitalization)
  - Bankruptcy or insolvency
  - Material breach of the merger agreement
  - Failure to pay within 90 days of the qualifying round
- **Subordination.** Subordinate to senior secured debt only. Pari passu with or senior to other unsecured obligations.

The structure above is standard for founder-deferred consideration in cash-light acquisitions. The penalties exist to make sure the deferred cash gets treated as a real obligation by Gridseat's future investors, not a soft promise that gets renegotiated away when a term sheet arrives.

### 7e. My ongoing role at Gridseat

- **Flexible — advisory or full-time operating, depending on what Gridseat needs.** I'm open to either. If Gridseat needs deep operational coverage on the rental/marketplace business unit, the insurance product, or partnership development, I can step in full-time. If Gridseat's leadership is already well-staffed and the right contribution is strategic, I'll take an advisory role and keep my time focused on the highest-leverage activities.
- **Reasonable candidate roles (working).** Head of Product, GM of the rental/marketplace business unit, or Chief Commercial / Strategy Officer leading partnerships (insurance, venues, race teams, private organizers).
- **Advisory shape (if that's the fit).** Defined scope (e.g. 5–10 hours/week), specific deliverables (insurance carrier relationship management, venue partnerships, paddock content/community development), and a 12-month minimum engagement at close.
- **Compensation.** If working role: market salary + retention equity grant in addition to the founder rollover equity. If advisory: advisory equity grant per market norms (typically 0.25–1.0% over 1–2 years) on top of the rollover.

But our strong preference is to go directly to acquisition; the prolonged uncertainty of a JV is more costly than just doing the deal.
